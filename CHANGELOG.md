No new segment, vendor, or dependency — both fields come from the same compact scrip master CSV `instruments.py` already parses. 37/37 tests passing.

ADR at `directives/adr/TASK-004_gamma-signals.md`. 12 new tests (73/73 total), ruff/mypy clean. See `reports/debug/TASK-004_debug-report.md`, `reports/qa/TASK-004_qa-report.md`.
